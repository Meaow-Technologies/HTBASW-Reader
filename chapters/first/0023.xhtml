Chapter 22.5: Author Notice: I'm Sorry... (July 21, 2017)

──────

Hello, this is Sing Shong.

I’m sorry to bring you the news I had feared.

After thinking it over for several days, I’ve decided to put How to Become a Star Writer on hiatus after Chapter 22, at least for the time being.

I’d been preparing this story for quite a long time, and because I wrote it with the aim of becoming a full-time author, I wanted to see it through to the end if possible... But due to various circumstances, I had no choice but to consider putting serialization on hold temporarily. (I’m sorry that it really has become a world after a hiatus... ㅠㅠ)

I sincerely thank and apologize to everyone who has enjoyed reading How to Become a Star Writer so far. I’d grown attached to this story, and I’ll make sure to return someday with a better version of it.

The weather has gotten very hot.

Please take care in the heat, and stay healthy always!

Sincerely,

Sing Shong

Footnotes:

“A world after a hiatus” parodies the title The World After the Fall, another work by the author.
